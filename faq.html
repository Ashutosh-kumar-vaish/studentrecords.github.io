Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How can I apply for admission?

You can apply by filling the online admission form available on the home page of the portal.

2. Which documents are required during admission?

You need JEE Main Scorecard, Class 10 & 12 Marksheet, TC, Migration Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Photographs, etc.

3. Do I need to pay any fee at the time of form submission?

No fee is required during initial online registration.

4. Can I edit the form after submission?

No. Once submitted, the form cannot be edited.

5. Is it mandatory to upload all required documents?

Yes, all required documents must be uploaded for verification.

6. How will I know if my application is successful?

You will see "Submitted Successfully" on the screen and may also receive an email confirmation.

7. Whom should I contact for admission-related queries?

You can visit the Contact Us page where email and phone details are provided.
